A widow with a weak heart, kept in the dark about her daughter's illness
because the truth might kill her.

She is the book's clearest piece of tragic irony: told nothing, she removes the
garlic from Lucy's room to let in fresh air, and undoes the only thing keeping
her daughter alive.
