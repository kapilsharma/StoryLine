Engaged to Lucy. Inherits the title in chapter 12, in the same week he
loses her.

He gives the first transfusion and drives the stake — the book gives both the
most intimate acts to the man with the strongest claim on her. Afterwards his
money and name are what actually make the hunt possible: he buys the launch and
opens doors that nobody else could.
