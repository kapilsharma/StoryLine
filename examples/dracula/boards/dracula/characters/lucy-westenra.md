Three proposals in one day in chapter 5; dead by chapter 12; staked in
chapter 16.

Lucy is the demonstration case. Everything the party later does to Dracula, they
learn by failing to do it for her — and her death is what converts a private
mystery into a hunt.

## Thread on this board

Note that her row does not end at chapter 12. Chapters 13–16 are the *Un-Dead*
Lucy, which is a different character wearing the same face; the board keeps them
on one row deliberately.
