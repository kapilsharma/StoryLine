A newly qualified solicitor sent east to close a property sale, and the
first person in the book to find out what he is dealing with.

His arc is really about **being believed**. He keeps a shorthand journal through
four chapters of captivity, escapes, and then spends months unable to tell
whether any of it happened — until Van Helsing reads the journal and takes it
seriously. From that point he is the party's researcher: it is Harker who traces
the fifty earth-boxes through carriers and ledgers.

## Thread on this board

Rows 1–4 are his alone. He drops out almost entirely between chapters 5 and 8,
which is worth seeing on the grid — the gap is the point.
