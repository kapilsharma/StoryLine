Present in far less of the book than memory suggests — he speaks at
length only in chapters 2–4, and after chapter 23 he is a cargo manifest and a
compass bearing rather than a character.

That absence is the design. Once he is in England he is tracked through what he
leaves behind: boxes of earth, shipping records, a madman's obedience, marks on a
throat.

## Thread on this board

Worth reading his row across the grid — it is mostly *gaps*, and the gaps are
where the dread lives.
