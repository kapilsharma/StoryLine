Seward's old teacher, called over from Amsterdam when Lucy's case stops
making medical sense.

He knows what is happening several chapters before he says so, which is the
engine of the middle of the book: he prescribes garlic and transfusions without
explaining why, loses Lucy partly because nobody obeys instructions they do not
understand, and only then tells the others what they are hunting.
