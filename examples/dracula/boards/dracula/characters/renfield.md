Seward's case study — a man who eats flies to feed to spiders, spiders to
feed to sparrows, and keeps a ledger of the lives he has absorbed.

Renfield is the book's early-warning system: his fits track Dracula's movements
several chapters before anyone connects them. He is also its moral hinge. He
invites the Count in, then refuses him Mina, and is killed for it.
