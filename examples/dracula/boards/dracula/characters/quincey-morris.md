The third of Lucy's suitors, refused in chapter 5 and a friend to
everyone concerned from that moment on.

He asks the questions the others are too polite to ask — he is the one who works
out that if four men have given blood, something has taken it. He dies at the
castle road, and the child born the following year carries his name.
