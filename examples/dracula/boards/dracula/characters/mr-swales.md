An old sailor who sits with Mina in the churchyard above the harbour and
tells her the gravestones are full of lies — most of the men named on them are at
the bottom of the sea.

Two scenes and a death, but he does real structural work: he establishes that
Whitby's dead are not reliably where they are said to be, immediately before a
ship arrives carrying something that should be dead and is not.
