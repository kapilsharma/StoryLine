Runs the lunatic asylum next door to Carfax — an accident of geography
that puts him inside the story before he knows there is one.

Refused by Lucy in chapter 5, and thereafter the book's sceptic: he records
everything on phonograph cylinders and believes none of it until he is taken to
the tomb and shown.
