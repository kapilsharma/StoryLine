Born a year to the day after Quincey Morris died, and named for him —
though Jonathan notes the boy's "bundle of names links all our little band of men
together".

## Why he is here but not on the board

Quincey appears in one paragraph of the closing Note, so he is not a row on the
timeline — he has no thread to draw. He exists as a character file purely so the
**Family** tab has a second generation to show.

That is the distinction ZN Story Line draws between *cast* and *plot*: a
character can live in a board's folder for family context without being a member
of the board's grid.
