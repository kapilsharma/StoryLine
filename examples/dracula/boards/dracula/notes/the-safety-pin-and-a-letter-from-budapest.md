Two small red punctures at Lucy's throat, which Mina — who fastened her
shawl in the dark — decides she must have made herself. Then Sister Agatha writes
from the hospital of St Joseph: Jonathan is alive and raving. Mina leaves the
same day, and leaves Lucy alone.
