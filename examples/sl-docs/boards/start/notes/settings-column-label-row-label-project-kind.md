Open the **Settings** tab. **Column levels** names the levels of your columns, outermost first — just "Chapter", or "Part" › "Chapter", or "Novel" › "Part" › "Chapter" › "Scene". The last level names the Timeline tab ("Chapters", "Scenes", "Sections"). **Row label** renames the Characters tab ("Characters", "Topics", "Phases").

Set **Project kind** to *General* if your rows are not people. **Card statuses** is the list a card's status is picked from — an icon and what it means for each, in dropdown order. Use ↑ ↓ to reorder, ✕ to remove, **+ Status** to add, **Reset to defaults** to get the original five back. Renaming a status or changing its icon updates every card; a removed one just stops showing. Press **Save project settings** — this one is not auto-saved.
