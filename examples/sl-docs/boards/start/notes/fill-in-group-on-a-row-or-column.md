The character form has a **Group** field: everything sharing a value collects under one heading on the board. Click a heading to collapse it — collapsed groups show a count of the cards hidden inside. Drag headings to reorder whole groups; drag a row header to move one row inside its group.

Columns group differently, because they can nest and carry notes: make a group on the **Timeline tab** and put columns inside it. Its heading appears over those columns; click the ▾ to fold it, or its name to open its note.
