```markdown
---
id: ch-4
label: "Chapter 4"
order: 4
summary: "Jonathan escapes the castle"
group: "Part One"
---
The last of the Transylvanian journal.
```

`order` is an integer and decides the column position. `summary` is the tooltip on the column header.
