A **slug** is the filename stem of a character, timeline unit or note — `mina.md`, `ch-4.md`. It doubles as the `id` in frontmatter.

A **uid** (`n_9f8e7d6c`) is a note's permanent identity, written once and never changed. A **card id** (`card-1a2b3c4d`) identifies a placement inside `board.json` and appears nowhere else.
