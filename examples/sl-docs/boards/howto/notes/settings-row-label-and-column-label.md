Call them "Topics" and "Sections", or "Products" and "Quarters" — whatever your grid actually holds.

**Column levels** is a list, outermost first. One level is a plain list of columns. Add more for *Novel*, *Part*, *Chapter*, *Scene* — the last level is what cards sit on, and each level above it can be a group with its own note. A level is named by its depth, so adding one above renames what is already at the top.
