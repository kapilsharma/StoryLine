Right-click a row header → **Hide row**. Right-click a column header to hide it outright.

Once a board has plot rows, two toggles appear in the board toolbar: **📋** hides every plot row and **👥** hides every character row — so you can drop to a structure-only or character-only view in one click. The choice is remembered per board.

To narrow a crowded board for a moment, click **🔍** in the board toolbar. A filter bar opens below the tabs with a **Characters** and a **Scenes** dropdown — untick what you don't want to see, or untick a whole group to drop its members at once. Filtering only changes the view: it touches nothing on disk, stacks on top of whatever is already hidden, and clears when you switch boards. Click **Clear filters** to bring everything back, or **🔍** again to close the bar.
