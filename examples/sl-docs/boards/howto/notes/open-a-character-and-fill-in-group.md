Characters tab → select → **Group**. Any text: "The Harkers", "Act 1", "Antagonists".

The same form has a **Row type**. Leave it on *Character* for people; switch it to *Plot (planning thread)* for a high-level row that is not a person — a transformation arc, the stakes, a beat you want laid across the timeline. Plot rows always sit above the character rows and stay off the family tree.
