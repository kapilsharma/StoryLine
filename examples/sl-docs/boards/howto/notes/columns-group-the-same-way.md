Columns group on the **Timeline tab**, and they can nest: a project can be *Novel › Part › Chapter › Scene*, or just chapters, whatever it needs.

Add a group with **+ Add part** (named after your outermost level; with more levels there is one button per level, e.g. **+ Add chapter** for a middle one — select the group it belongs in first and it starts inside it), then drag a column onto it — or pick it in the column's **Inside** field. On the board the group becomes a heading over its columns; click the ▾ to fold it away, or click its name to open its note.
