The Timeline tab is the list of timeline units — your chapters, scenes, acts or stages — with the same select-and-edit shape as the Characters tab. Groups appear as a tree: each group with its columns indented beneath it.

It is named after the project's deepest **Column level**. From here you can add, rename and reorder groups and columns, drag one onto a group to move it inside, break a column into smaller parts, and delete a group — which first lists everything inside it, notes included, and asks you to confirm.
