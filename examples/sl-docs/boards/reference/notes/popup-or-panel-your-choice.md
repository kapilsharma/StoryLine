**Settings → Application → Note opened from a board** decides what a click on a card or a row header opens.

*Popup over the board* is the default: a read-only preview in the middle of the window, with **Edit** for the fullscreen editor. Quick to open, quick to dismiss — but on a busy board the grid showing through behind it is a lot to look at.

*Panel beside the board* opens the note down the right-hand side instead. The board keeps the rest of the page and stays readable, and the note is edited where it is read: click any line and it becomes Markdown, **Esc** renders it again. Drag the panel's left edge to change the split; double-click it for half and half.

Both stay supported. The setting is an app preference, so it follows you between projects.
