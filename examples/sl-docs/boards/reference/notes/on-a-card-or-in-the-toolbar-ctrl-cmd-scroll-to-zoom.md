A card whose title is long enough to need it gets a **⤢** button; the toolbar **⤢** expands or collapses every card at once.

A row header whose name does not fit in two lines gets its own **⤢**, top right, when you hover over it. Row names expand one at a time — the toolbar button is for cards.

Hold **Ctrl** (or **Cmd**) and scroll to zoom, between 50% and 200%.
