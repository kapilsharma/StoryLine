title: Hiding a column inside a span shortens it on screen
boards:
  - reference
tags:
  - board
created: '2026-08-17'
---
A span anchors to the first and last *visible* column in its range. Hide a column in the middle and the card simply closes up; hide the whole range and the card disappears until you unhide.

Nothing is lost — the stored span is unchanged.
