On a *story* project the form also carries **Family**, **Gender**, **Born**, **Died**, **Maiden name**, **Father**, **Mother**, **Spouse** and **Children** — everything the family tree draws from. Dates may be a year, a year-month, or a full date.

**Custom fields** let you add any key and value you like; they are kept in the file and shown back to you.
