Because boards are independent, the same person appearing in two boards is two separate character files that know nothing about each other.

Edit one and the other does not change. If you want one cast across several storylines, keep them on one board and use groups or presets to separate the strands.

Deleting a board loses its card placements; its notes and character files stay on disk.
