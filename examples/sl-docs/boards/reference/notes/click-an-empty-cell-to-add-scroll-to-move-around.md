Clicking an empty cell creates a card there. The board scrolls in both directions, with row and column headers staying put.

Right-click a row header for its menu; right-click a column header to hide that column immediately.

The row-header column can be resized: hover over its right-hand edge, anywhere down the board, and drag when the cursor changes. It stops at a readable minimum and at half the visible board, so the plot always keeps its half. Double-click the edge to put it back to the default.
