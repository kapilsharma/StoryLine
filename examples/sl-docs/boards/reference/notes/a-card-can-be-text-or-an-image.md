Every card starts as **Text**. To make it an **image card** — a map, a mood picture, a character sketch — open its note in the side panel or the fullscreen editor and choose **Image** at the start of the tags row. A picture box appears to the left of the tags and hover text, with a chooser under it: pick one of the pictures already on this board, or **Upload new image…** to add a PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, SVG or AVIF. An upload is copied into the board's `assets/` folder, the same as an image in a note; picking one already there copies nothing, so reuse it rather than uploading it again. A file dropped on the box is uploaded straight away.

The card becomes an image card once a picture is chosen. Click the picture to open the chooser again and change it; choose **Text** to turn it back into a text card — the file stays in `assets/`.

On the board the picture is drawn at the **Image card height** setting and keeps its shape; a row holding an image card grows to fit it, and rows without one stay compact. **Image card title** puts the title **on the right** of the picture, **below** it, or shows **no text** — then only the status, 📝 and 🔗 markers sit over the picture's corner. Both are under **Settings → General**. The popup shows the picture above the tags; revision mode hides it with the title.
