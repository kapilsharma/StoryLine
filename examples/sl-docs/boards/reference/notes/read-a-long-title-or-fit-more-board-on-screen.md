A card shows as much of its title as fits. Expanding grows the card to show the whole thing; zooming scales the entire board.

Row names work the same way. A name in the fixed first column wraps to two lines, and expanding opens it out in full — the row grows to make room.

All of this is about reading, not about content — nothing on disk changes.
